So, I have a funny(ish) story from the past weekend (I’ll get back to the "ish" part at the end).

On Saturday, got an email from Buy Me a Coffee saying that "our moderation team has suspended your account due to risk concerns". I was like, WTH? I’ve been barely using the platform. On the content creation side, just a couple of posts here and there, on the same subjects as always. What gives?

Contacted support, and they replied that "our moderation team flagged your account for illegal content such as IPTV which is against BMC's terms". Which got me, again, WTH?

I assume they think I’ve built some sort of application that pirates IPTV streams or something like that, but that’s shortsighted to say the least, given I’m pretty sure there is much more people building legitimate IPTV and related applications, than illegal ones.

I built IPTV applications a long time ago, when my job was building them for a legitimate TV provider, one of the biggest in Portugal ([MEO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MEO_(telecommunication_company))) 🤷‍♂️.

Now, was this an automated process looking blindly for keywords, or some eager moderation team member that has no idea that IPTV isn’t synonymous with pirating? No clue, but in either case, doesn’t make any sense. Maybe with the football world cup imminent (or some other sports event) they are more sensitive to these kinds of things, though that doesn’t justify this sort of mistake.

So far, I've not heard back from them after trying to explain to them their mistake. I replied not really because I care about the account, if I'm honest all of this has made me want to cancel it anyway, but this sort of carelessness for the users makes me mad. Given the radio silence, I assume they'll close my account soon, but I'll update this post with the outcome.

Now, to close with the funny(ish) bit.

I’ve had the blog since 2016, started creating open source code samples by the same time, created the YouTube channel in 2017, and created my Buy Me a Coffee account in 2022. During all this time, I got a total of… 1 person "buying me a coffee".

All of this to say (besides being a bit sad that for all the hundreds of thousands of views of my content, plus usage of my open source code and samples, only one person thought it was a worth contributing, but I digress), I don’t depend on the income from Buy Me a Coffee.

If I did depend on that income though, this story wouldn’t be funny, it would be terrifying, because my livelihood could be in question, just because someone, or something, thought IPTV = illegal pirates.
